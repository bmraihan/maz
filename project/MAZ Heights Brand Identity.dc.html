03 — VOICE
Plain, priced, and on the record.
Say: “Fixed written quote after the survey.” “Weekly photo updates.” “Building control sign-off included.”
Avoid: “bespoke luxury solutions”, “passionate about quality”, exclamation marks, stock enthusiasm.
Rule: every claim is checkable — a number, a certificate, or a named customer.
